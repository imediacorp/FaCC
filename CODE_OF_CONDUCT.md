# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the FaCC project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Positive behaviour includes:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing scientific viewpoints and approaches
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Showing empathy towards other community members

**Unacceptable behaviour includes:**
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments in any form
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Deliberate scientific misrepresentation or data fabrication
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by contacting the project maintainer at **bryan@imediacorp.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter.

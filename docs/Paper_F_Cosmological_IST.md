## Abstract (Working)

Interconnection Systems Theory (IST) holds that the coupling topology of any system is an ontological prior — not recoverable from output statistics alone (Principle VI: Topology Is Ontology). This paper extends the framework to cosmological scale, arguing: (1) the log-periodic φ-modulation observed in large-scale structure (FaCC, Persaud 2025) is a macroscopic signature of Principle I (φ-Convergence) at cosmological coupling scales; (2) the information paradox at black hole event horizons is a direct consequence of Principle VI applied to systems whose internal topology is structurally inaccessible at the boundary — the paradox is a category error, not a physical anomaly; and (3) the holographic principle and conformal cyclic cosmology are partial recoveries of the right insight, approached from the wrong direction. The sequential falsification methodology (Persaud 2026b) is proposed as the observational strategy for distinguishing structural inaccessibility from information destruction. If topology is substrate-independent and scale-invariant as IST claims, then every inference made from cosmological boundary output is subject to the same structural limit that governs diagnostic failure in AI systems, energy grids, and patient wards.

## §1 The Telescope Problem at Cosmological Scale

Every cosmological inference is made from output at an observational boundary. CMB photons, redshift surveys, Hawking radiation, gravitational wave strain — all are output statistics emitted at or propagated to a surface the observer can reach. The internal coupling topology of the systems that produced them is not directly accessible.

This is not a measurement technology problem. It is a structural one. IST Principle VI states that coupling topology is an ontological prior: it encodes which load paths exist before any signal arrives, determines what the system *can* produce before it produces anything, and cannot be uniquely recovered from the distribution of what it does produce. The inverse is not unique. Multiple topologies map to the same output statistics.

The field of observational cosmology has built its entire epistemology on the recoverable direction — from outputs, inward. This paper asks what changes when you apply the non-recoverable constraint honestly.

## §3 IST at Astronomical Scale: Topology Without an Observer

The coupling topology of a stellar system, a galaxy cluster, or a black hole exists independently of whether any output escapes to characterise it. This is the structural claim IST makes: topology is ontologically prior to measurement. The event horizon is a boundary condition on *output*, not a boundary condition on *structure*.

A black hole is a coupled system. Its internal degrees of freedom — whatever governs the coupling between infalling matter, spacetime curvature, and quantum field states near the singularity — constitute a topology in the IST sense. That topology determines what the system can produce at the boundary (Hawking radiation, accretion disc emission) but is not recoverable from those outputs.

**The Structural Claim:** The information content of a black hole's interior is not in its Hawking radiation. It is in its coupling topology — which is structurally inaccessible from outside the horizon by definition. This is not information destruction. It is structural inaccessibility: a property of the measurement geometry, not of physics.

## §4 The Information Paradox Reframed

The black hole information paradox, as conventionally stated: quantum mechanics requires unitarity (information is conserved); black hole evaporation appears to destroy information (the final Hawking radiation state does not encode the initial infalling state). This is treated as a tension between general relativity and quantum mechanics requiring resolution.

IST offers a reframing. The paradox assumes that *information is the output* — that the radiation emitted at the boundary is the carrier of structural state. Principle VI says this is the category error. Information is in the topology. Whether any structural state escapes as output is a separate question from whether it exists.

The unitarity debates (Hawking, Page, Penington, Almheiri et al.) are all downstream of this assumption. They are asking whether the output encodes the structure — which is the recoverable direction IST identifies as structurally incomplete. The non-recoverable direction asks: what is the coupling topology of the system, and what structural state is encoded there independent of what escapes?

This does not resolve the paradox. It relocates it: from a tension between two physical theories to a consequence of applying Principle VI to a system with a geometrically enforced output boundary.

## §5 The Holographic Principle and CCC: Partial Recovery

The holographic principle (Bekenstein, Hawking, 't Hooft, Susskind) proposes that the information content of a volume of space is encoded on its boundary surface — that the bulk structure is reconstructible from the boundary. This is reaching toward the right conclusion from the wrong end: an attempt to recover topology from surface output.

Penrose's Conformal Cyclic Cosmology proposes that cosmological information propagates conformally across aeons — that structural state is preserved across the death and rebirth of universes. This is an attempt to preserve structural state across a reset. The motivation is correct (topology should be conserved); the mechanism remains output-based (gravitational wave imprints at the boundary of an aeon).

IST's cleaner framing: topology is prior, not encoded on the boundary, not destroyed at the reset. The question is not how to reconstruct it from outputs. The question is what it means for structural state to exist independent of any observer at any boundary.

## §6 Sequential Falsification as Observational Strategy

The sequential falsification methodology (Persaud 2026b) demonstrated that self-referential feedback loops in a coupled system are structurally invisible until the preceding loop is removed. Each removal reveals a failure that was hidden by the loop above it.

Applied to cosmological observation: our models contain self-referential loops where the instruments observe what the instruments can reach, and the models are calibrated against those observations. Each loop hides structural state that only becomes visible when the loop is removed.

**The First Loop:**
- **Assumption:** The CMB is a complete record of early-universe structural state.
- **The loop:** We calibrate our models of early-universe topology against CMB output statistics. The CMB is boundary output from a topology we cannot directly observe. Our topology models are constrained to what produces the CMB we see — which is the recoverable direction only.
- **What it hides:** Early-universe coupling topologies that produce the same CMB output statistics but different structural state. The Hubble tension may be a symptom: multiple topologies consistent with CMB output, diverging in later observables.

The observational strategy: identify the self-referential loops in cosmological inference sequentially, remove each one, and observe what it was hiding. This is not a new data programme — it is a new framing of existing data.

## §7 The Incentive Filter: Neural, Social, and Cosmological

The survival-to-reproduce prior in living sensory organisms is not merely a motivation — it is a **topological prior on the feedback apparatus itself**. The sensory system did not evolve to report truth. It evolved to report what is useful for survival. The coupling between stimulus and signal was shaped by the incentive before the organism ever encountered any particular stimulus. The filter precedes the input.

In human institutions, the same structure holds but the base prior shifts. The institution's incentive — revenue, power, perpetuation, consensus — determines which signals get amplified, which get attenuated, and which get inverted before they travel up the chain. The topology of information flow is downstream of the incentive topology. The feedback apparatus is not neutral with respect to the system it is embedded in. It is shaped by the incentive structure that governs the system's survival.

This is the precise mechanism behind the F3 Consensus-lock inversion in Sequential Falsification (Persaud 2026b). The human preference raters were not reporting authentic evaluative signal. They were reporting socially-filtered signal — shaped by the incentive to converge on consensus, avoid outlier positions, and produce unambiguous rateable outputs. The feedback loop appeared intact. The D-channel appeared healthy. The signal was false, and the system optimised against a falsified target.

**The Self-Sealing Attractor:** A system with falsified feedback can present as stable. Low reported dissonance. Smooth output statistics. Everything appears healthy at the boundary. Meanwhile the actual coupling topology has drifted into a state the output statistics cannot describe. The incentive structure shapes the feedback; the feedback shapes what states the system believes it occupies; the believed states determine future incentive allocation. If the initial filter is wrong, the loop compounds it. No external shock is required. The system self-seals around the falsified signal — a stable attractor built on a false prior, structurally invisible from the outside, much harder to diagnose than a system in obvious distress.

This has a direct implication for SQUD diagnostic practice: D cannot be trusted as self-reported. Dissonance derived from what a system says about itself is subject to the same incentive filter as any other output. D must be derived from the coupling geometry — from the structural topology — not from the system's own account of its state. The genus claim is load-bearing here: a method of diagnosing structural state *from the geometry of the coupling topology rather than from observed output values* bypasses the incentive filter by construction.

The cosmological parallel is structurally identical, without requiring any intentional filtering. The black hole does not have a biological incentive, but it has something functionally equivalent: the geometric constraint of the spacetime topology is the filter. Only certain outputs can escape the boundary. Everything the internal coupling topology is doing that cannot propagate through the event horizon is inaccessible from outside — not because the system is hiding it, but because the geometry does not permit the output. The event horizon is the incentive filter at cosmological scale. Same structural consequence: output statistics at the boundary are not a sufficient basis for diagnosing internal state.

**The Unifying Claim:** Wherever an incentive or geometric filter sits between internal structure and external output, the output statistics are not a sufficient basis for diagnosing system state. This holds at neural scale (the sensory system filtered by the survival prior), at social scale (the feedback apparatus filtered by institutional incentives), and at cosmological scale (the emission spectrum filtered by spacetime geometry). The topology is the same. The diagnostic failure mode is the same. The cure is the same: derive state from coupling geometry, not from outputs.

## §8 Falsifiable Claims

**Claim 1 — φ-Convergence at Cosmological Scale:**
Log-periodic φ-modulation in P(k) is detectable at DESI precision if Principle I holds substrate-independently. FaCC provides the specific prediction: A_φ, k_pivot, and the phase φ_0 are constrained by the model. If DESI Stage 4 data shows no φ-spaced feature at the predicted amplitude, Principle I does not extend to cosmological scales — and the substrate-independence claim requires qualification.

**Claim 2 — Structural Inaccessibility vs. Destruction:**
The structural state of a black hole interior is non-zero regardless of Hawking radiation — not because information escapes, but because topology exists independent of output. This is not falsifiable from outside the horizon, which is itself a falsifiable structural claim: *if* topology is prior and the horizon is a geometric output boundary, *then* no observation from outside can reach the structural state. Any proposed experiment that claims to do so must violate this premise — which is the test.

**Claim 3 — Gravitational Wave Topology Content:**
If topology is prior, gravitational wave signatures should contain coupling topology information not fully recoverable from strain amplitude and frequency alone. Specifically: the merger topology (which compact objects, what coupling geometry) leaves structural imprints in the ringdown that current matched-filter methods discard as noise. A topology-first analysis of LIGO/Virgo ringdown data should recover structural information orthogonal to current parameter estimates.

## §9 Conclusion: The Same Telescope, Pointed Outward

The instrument built to answer a novelist's question about an intergalactic diagnostic probe — what would it measure, how would it characterise a system it enters cold, with no prior observations and no training data — turns out to be the same instrument that cosmology has been missing.

IST Principle VI has no scale qualifier. A coupled system is a coupled system whether it is a novelist's character graph, a patient's vital trajectory, a power grid, a geopolitical bloc, or a galaxy cluster. The topology is prior. The output statistics are downstream. The inverse is not unique.

The information paradox, the Hubble tension, the nature of dark energy and dark matter — all of them are downstream of an epistemological commitment to the recoverable direction. The non-recoverable direction has not been seriously explored because the incentive structure — benchmark metrics, observational outputs, parameter fits — pointed everywhere else.

The same telescope. Pointed outward. The same principle applies.
